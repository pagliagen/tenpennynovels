# Revenue Projections & Financial Analysis

## Executive Summary

**Business Model**: Open Source + Managed Hosting + Add-ons

**Revenue Streams**: Subscription (MRR) + One-time add-ons + Future marketplace

## Unit Economics

### Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

**Year 1** (mostly organic):
- GitHub/Reddit/SEO: €0-5 per customer
- Word of mouth: €0

**Blended CAC**: €3/customer

**Year 2-3** (paid marketing):
- Google Ads: €30-50 per customer
- Social media: €20-40 per customer
- Content marketing: €10-20 per customer

**Blended CAC**: €25/customer

### Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)

**STARTER**:
- ARPU: €15/mese
- Avg lifetime: 12 months
- Add-ons: €40 once
- **LTV**: €220

**PRO**:
- ARPU: €45/mese
- Avg lifetime: 18 months
- Add-ons: €60 once
- **LTV**: €870

**ENTERPRISE**:
- ARPU: €119/mese
- Avg lifetime: 24 months
- Add-ons: €150 once
- **LTV**: €3,006

**Blended LTV** (weighted):
- 60% STARTER + 30% PRO + 10% ENTERPRISE
- **Blended LTV**: €644

### LTV/CAC Ratio

**Year 1**: €644 / €3 = **214:1** (exceptional!)
**Year 2-3**: €644 / €25 = **25:1** (excellent!)

**Target**: >3:1 is good, >5:1 is excellent

### Payback Period

**Year 1**: <1 month (instant)
**Year 2-3**: 1 month (STARTER), <1 month (PRO/ENT)

**Target**: <12 months acceptable, <6 months good, <3 months excellent

## Churn Analysis

### Churn Assumptions

**Year 1**: 5% monthly
- Higher churn (experimentation phase)
- Some customers try but don't stick

**Year 2**: 3% monthly
- Product-market fit found
- Better onboarding
- More features

**Year 3+**: 2% monthly
- Mature product
- Strong community
- High switching cost (data, time invested)

### Churn Prevention Strategies

1. **Onboarding**: Dedicated wizard for new customers
2. **Engagement**: Weekly email with tips
3. **Support**: Fast response times
4. **Features**: Continuous improvement
5. **Community**: Discord, forum, events
6. **Lock-in**: More data = harder to leave
7. **Pricing**: Fair pricing (no "gotchas")

### Revenue Churn vs Customer Churn

If high-value customers (ENTERPRISE) churn less than low-value (STARTER):
- Customer churn: 3%
- Revenue churn: 2% (better!)

Goal: Negative revenue churn (upsells > losses)

## Investment Thesis

### Why This Business Will Succeed

1. **Proven Model**: Open source + hosted = GitLab, Ghost, Discourse
2. **Niche Market**: Victorian RPG = passionate fans
3. **Low CAC**: Organic growth via GitHub, Reddit, community
4. **High LTV**: Sticky product (data, time invested)
5. **Scalable**: Hosted service scales linearly
6. **Defensible**: Community, content, network effects
7. **Multiple Revenue Streams**: Subscription + add-ons + marketplace

### Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| **Slow adoption** | High | Start self-hosted (zero cost), build community |
| **High churn** | High | Focus on onboarding, engagement, support |
| **Competitor** | Medium | First-mover advantage, open source moat |
| **Tech issues** | Medium | Start small, scale gradually, monitoring |
| **Bot AI costs** | Low | Pass costs to customer (included in PRO) |
